Noise → image (scrub t)

The other half of generative ML

Did you know?

Jonathan Ho's DDPM (2020) took a 2015 thermodynamics analogy and made it beat GANs on FID — within two years, image generation was diffusion's field.

In the real world

Images, video, audio, protein structure, robot policies — anywhere output must be high-dimensional and multimodal, this family won. Text stays autoregressive… for now.

Autoregressive models emit one token at a time; diffusion denoises the whole output at once. The panel runs the reverse process: pure noise on the left edge of the scrub, structure condensing out as $t \to 0$.

Learn the noise you added

Did you know?

The training objective is almost embarrassingly simple: pick a random noise level, add Gaussian noise, and train the network to predict which noise was added — `ddpm-loss`. That's the entire supervision signal.

Forward: corrupt $x_0$ to $x_t$ by schedule. Learn: $\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t) \approx \epsilon$. Sample: start at pure noise, peel it off in steps — DDPM stochastic, DDIM/ODE deterministic and ~20× fewer steps.

Do it small: latent diffusion

Did you know?

Stable Diffusion's trick was compression, not cleverness: denoise a 64×64 latent instead of 512² pixels — ~64× less work, consumer-GPU friendly.

In the real world

Same trick runs everywhere: video latents, audio latents — compress to where details live cheaply, diffuse there, decode once at the end.

A VAE encoder squeezes pixels to a smooth latent; diffusion runs there; the decoder expands the result once. The expensive part got 64× cheaper for nearly free quality.

Steering: guidance and flow matching

Did you know?

CFG runs the model twice per step — once with the prompt, once without — and extrapolates away from the unconditional answer. The "guidance scale" slider is that subtraction.

In the real world

Flow matching + diffusion transformers (DiT) is the 2024+ recipe — SD3, Flux, Sora-class video. Straighter paths, fewer steps, better scaling.

Text conditioning comes in through cross-attention; guidance sharpens it. Then the field swapped the formulation itself: learn the velocity field from noise to data — same family, less stochastic baggage.
