Diffusion and flow generation

MapPlaybackNotes

Iterative denoising and flow matching — how images, video and audio are generated.

Statement

Generation by iterated denoising: forward — destroy data by adding Gaussian noise over $T$ steps; learn $\epsilon_\theta(x_t, t)$ to predict the noise (ddpm-loss), then reverse — start from pure noise and denoise into a sample. latent-diffusion runs it in a VAE latent; flow-matching learns the velocity field directly; classifier-free-guidance controls prompt adherence.

In my own words

One paragraph, as few symbols as possible. Written by me, not copied.

Why the parent needs it

modern-machine-learning — generative media is the other half of the field: images, video, audio, and increasingly robot actions. Different objective, different architecture family (U-Nets/diffusion transformers vs decoder stacks), different sampling economics — and the modality output head for multimodal-models.

Teardown

  1. Forward process. $x_t = \sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0 + \sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon$ — a fixed corruption schedule, no learning.
  2. The objective. Train eps-noise predictor with ddpm-loss — literally "guess the noise that was added", one per noise level.
  3. Reverse sampling. DDPM: stochastic reverse chain; DDIM/ ODE-solvers: deterministic, 20–50 steps instead of 1000.
  4. Latent space. latent-diffusion: compress pixels → denoise the latent → decode. 8× spatial compression is why SD ran on consumer GPUs.
  5. Conditioning. Text via cross-attention (CLIP/T5 embeddings); classifier-free-guidance extrapolates away from an unconditional pass — the "creativity vs prompt-faithfulness" dial.
  6. Flow matching. flow-matching: learn $dx/dt = v_\theta$ — the straight-path reformulation; DiT + flow is the 2024+ default (SD3, Flux, Sora-class).

Worked example

Latent diffusion on a 512² image:

  • VAE: 512² RGB → 64×64×4 latent — 64× fewer values to denoise.
  • 50 DDIM steps ≈ $50 \times$ one UNet pass ≈ a few seconds on a consumer GPU — vs 1000+ pixel-space steps for 2020-era DDPM.
  • Guidance 7.5: $\hat\epsilon = \epsilon_{uncond} + 7.5(\epsilon_{cond}
  • \epsilon_{uncond})$ — the prompt pulls 7.5× harder.
