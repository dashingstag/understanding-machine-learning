A benchmark's life cycle

The weirdest weakness in the field

Did you know?

Charles Goodhart's law — "when a measure becomes a target it ceases to be a good measure" (statement) — was coined for economics in 1975 and describes benchmark marketing better than any ML paper.

In the real world

Every frontier release is a chart of numbers — and contamination audits routinely find the test set inside the training corpus. The numbers are the weakest part of the paper.

The pipeline that makes trillion-parameter models is engineering-grade; the instruments used to measure them are jury-rigged. This page is about what the scores actually mean.

The benchmark suite, and its churn

Did you know?

MMLU launched 2020 with GPT-3 at 44%. By 2024 frontier models were at ~90% and the field minted MMLU-Pro and GPQA to get headroom back — benchmark half-life is now ~2 years.

Knowledge (MMLU), reasoning (GSM8K→MATH→GPQA), code (HumanEval→SWE-bench), safety — the suite keeps getting replaced as models saturate it. The panel traces a benchmark's life: launch → climb → saturate → retire.

When there's no answer key: judges

Did you know?

Chatbot Arena is the workaround for un-scorable tasks: humans vote pairwise on anonymous answers, and Elo does the rest — the closest thing the field has to a taste measurement.

In the real world

LLM-as-judge scaled the same trick: a strong model grades the answer. Fast and cheap — but judges prefer verbosity, their own family's style, and confident nonsense phrased well.

Open-ended capability (writing, analysis, agent trajectories) has no answer key — so evaluation itself became a modeling problem.

How to read a headline number

Did you know?

The same model's score can swing 5+ points on prompt format, few-shot choice, and answer extraction — reproducibility in evals is a real research area, not a footnote.

In the real world

The checklist when you see a number: was the test set contaminated? Self-reported or third-party? Few-shot? Judge model? Which version of the suite?

Evals measure a distribution of behaviours, not a point — the honest summary is a procedure, not a score.
