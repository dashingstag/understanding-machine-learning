Direct preference optimization (DPO)

MapNotes

The closed-form alternative that skips the reward model — now the cheap default for preference tuning.

Statement

The closed-form shortcut around rlhf: under the optimal-policy form of the KL-regularized objective, the reward can be written in terms of the policy itself — so dpo-loss trains pi-policy directly on preference pairs, no reward model, no sampling, no RL. A supervised loss with an RLHF-shaped optimum.

In my own words

One paragraph, as few symbols as possible. Written by me, not copied.

Why the parent needs it

post-training — most open models align with DPO-family losses rather than full PPO: simpler (one model + frozen reference), cheaper, more stable. It's the pragmatic majority case, and its derivations explain what RLHF is really optimizing.

Teardown

  1. The trick. Rearrange the KL-regularized optimum: $r(x,y) = \beta \log \frac{\pi(y|x)}{\pi_{ref}(y|x)} + \beta\log Z(x)$ — reward becomes a log-ratio of policies; the partition term cancels inside the Bradley–Terry difference.
  2. The loss. dpo-loss: maximize $\log\sigma\big(\beta[\log\frac{\pi(y_w)}{\pi_{ref}(y_w)} - \log\frac{\pi(y_l)}{\pi_{ref}(y_l)}]\big)$ — push chosen up, rejected down, relative to the frozen reference.
  3. beta as the leash. Same role as the KL coefficient in rlhf-obj: small β drifts far (length bias, mode collapse); large β stays near SFT and learns little.
  4. Variants. IPO (fixes overfitting), KTO (binary signals, no pairs), ORPO (folds SFT+pref into one loss), SimPO (reference-free) — the DPO-family zoo.
  5. Known failure modes. Length exploitation, decreased diversity, sensitivity to pair quality — and it can't learn from unlabeled exploration the way on-policy RL can (verifiable-rewards).

Worked example

Pair: chosen log-ratio $\log\frac{\pi(y_w)}{\pi_{ref}(y_w)} = -0.2$, rejected $= -1.1$, β = 0.1:

  • Margin: $0.1 \times (-0.2 + 1.1) = 0.09$ → loss $-\log\sigma(0.09) \approx 0.69$.
  • After updates make the margin $0.5$: loss ≈ 0.47 — the pair is "learned" once the policy prefers chosen by a β-scaled gap over the reference.
