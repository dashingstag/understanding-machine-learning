Interpretability and safety

MapPlaybackNotes

Mechanistic interpretability, sparse autoencoders, probes and steering — plus misuse and alignment risk.

Statement

Reverse-engineering what a trained model computes: probing (does a layer encode property X), sparse-autoencoders (decompose activations into named features), activation-steering (edit behaviours by adding directions mid-forward-pass), and the residual-stream lens from residual-normalization. "Mechanistic" = causal claims about circuits, not correlations.

In my own words

One paragraph, as few symbols as possible. Written by me, not copied.

Why the parent needs it

modern-machine-learning — we deploy systems we cannot yet audit: understanding what a model actually computes is the long-term route to trusting it, and it takes the whole stack as its subject — attention heads (attention-mechanism), FFN storage (feedforward-layer), the stream (residual-normalization), the head (unembedding).

Teardown

  1. The problem. Neurons are superposed — one unit fires on French text AND code AND parentheses; polysemanticity defeats naive neuroscience.
  2. Probing. probing: train a linear classifier on activations — "does layer 12 encode tense?" Cheap, correlational, the oldest instrument.
  3. SAEs. sparse-autoencoders: dictionary-learn features over the activations — sparse codes disentangle superposition into nameable features (Golden Gate Claude's famous feature).
  4. Steering. activation-steering: add a feature direction to the residual stream → behaviour changes; the causal test of interpretability claims.
  5. Circuits. Attention-head programs (induction heads: copy previous-pattern) — the micro-mechanisms behind macro behaviour; Transformer Circuits thread.
  6. Safety stakes. Alignment faking, deceptive CoT, unfaithful reasoning (chain-of-thought faithfulness) — interpretability is where these become checkable claims rather than vibes.

Worked example

Induction head (Olsson et al. 2022): pattern [A][B] … [A] → head attends from second [A] to the token after first [A] = [B] — in-context learning's micro-mechanism, causally verified by ablation: kill the head, ICL scores drop.

Open questions

  • Can interpretability scale to the frontier (100B+ models, long contexts) — or does it stay a small-model science?
  • Causal faithfulness: do SAE features correspond to real mechanisms or post-hoc readouts? — the field's deepest open question.
