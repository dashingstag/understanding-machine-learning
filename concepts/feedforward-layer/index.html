Feed-forward layer

MapPlaybackNotes

The two-layer perceptron inside every block — roughly two-thirds of a transformer's parameters and most of its factual storage.

Statement

The position-wise two-layer perceptron inside every transformer block — see ffn: expand from d-model $d$ to $4d$ (gated variants: ~$8d/3$), apply sigma-act (ReLU/GELU/SiLU), project back. Identical weights at every position; ~⅔ of a model's n-params lives here.

In my own words

One paragraph, as few symbols as possible. Written by me, not copied.

Why the parent needs it

transformer-architecture — attention only routes information between positions; the FFN is where each position's representation is actually transformed. interpretability work (Geva et al. 2021) finds factual memories stored as key–value pairs in FFN matrices — "the store" to attention's "router".

Teardown

  1. Shape. $x \mapsto W_2\,\sigma(W_1 x + b_1) + b_2$, $W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{4d \times d}$ — two fat matmuls.
  2. Role. Attention mixes; FFN computes. Each position is processed independently — the FFN can't see other tokens, which is why it must happen after attention has gathered context.
  3. Activations. ReLU → GELU (GPT-2) → SiLU/SwiGLU (Llama-family): gated variants split the up-projection in two and multiply — $\text{SwiGLU}(x) = (\text{Swish}(xW) \otimes xV)\,W_2$.
  4. Where knowledge lives. Probing and causal interventions implicate mid-stack FFNs in factual recall (e.g., "the Eiffel Tower is in Paris" firing specific neurons) — see probing, sparse-autoencoders.
  5. The MoE hook. mixture-of-experts is just the FFN replicated $E$ times with a gate-g router choosing — same shape, sparse activation.

Worked example

GPT-2 small block: $d = 768$, FFN inner $= 4d = 3072$.

  • $W_1$: $768 \times 3072 \approx 2.36$M, $W_2$ same $\Rightarrow$ $\approx 4.72$M params vs attention's $\approx 2.36$M — exactly the ⅔ split quoted across this map.
  • Per token FLOPs $\approx 2 \times 4.72$M $\approx 9.4$M multiply-adds — cheap alone, dominant over 12 blocks.
