Supervised fine-tuning (SFT)

MapNotes

Stage one of every pipeline — imitation learning on curated demonstrations before any preference step.

Statement

Stage one of post-training: continue next-token-prediction training on a small curated set of (instruction, ideal-response) demonstrations — typically 10³–10⁵ examples. Same cross-entropy machinery, new distribution: the model learns chat format, tone, and the assistant/refusal boundary.

In my own words

One paragraph, as few symbols as possible. Written by me, not copied.

Why the parent needs it

post-training — preference stages (RLHF/DPO) shape which good response is produced; SFT installs the response format itself. It's also the cheapest capability lever known: LIMA showed ~1,000 top-quality examples can surface competence a base model already had.

Teardown

  1. Format over facts. SFT mainly teaches how to respond — style, structure, role tokens — not new knowledge; hallucinations often trace to it teaching the model to answer rather than say "I don't know" (Gekhman et al. 2024).
  2. Data. Human demonstrations (InstructGPT: contractor-written) or distilled teacher outputs (Alpaca-style self-instruct); quality ≫ quantity.
  3. Chat markup. Special tokens (<|im_start|>, roles) are trained here — the control channel tool-calling and agents-and-tool-use reuse.
  4. Continual-learning tension. Fine-tuning drifts from base capabilities; mitigations: low LR, few epochs, KL-anchored losses — the "alignment tax" debate.

Worked example

LIMA (Zhou et al. 2023): 65B model + 1,000 curated examples beat crowd-favourite Alpaca (52k distilled examples) on helpfulness — the canonical "less is more" datapoint for SFT quality.
