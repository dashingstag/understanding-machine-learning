Mixture of experts

MapPlaybackNotes

Sparse routing: each token activates a few of many expert feed-forward networks — capacity without proportional compute.

Statement

Replace the dense feedforward-layer with $E$ expert FFNs plus a learned gate-g router that sends each token to the top-$k$ experts — moe-layer. Parameters grow ~$E\times$ while per-token compute stays flat: capacity without proportional flop cost. Switch Transformer, Mixtral, DeepSeek-V3 and most frontier open models are MoE.

In my own words

One paragraph, as few symbols as possible. Written by me, not copied.

Why the parent needs it

modern-machine-learning — MoE set the price/performance curve of the whole field: Mixtral-8×7B-quality on ~13B activated params changed what "small" means, and DeepSeek-V3's MoE recipe reset expectations of open-model capability. Sparsity is the main trick scaling-laws didn't see coming.

Teardown

  1. The swap. Dense FFN → $E$ expert FFNs (same shape) + router. Per token: gate → top-$k$ experts → weighted sum — moe-layer.
  2. The router problem. expert-routing: softmax gates over experts, top-$k$ keep — discrete choice made differentiable by scaling the expert output by the gate score.
  3. Load balancing. Tokens flock to favourite experts → auxiliary loss penalizing skew (Switch), or bias-free balancing (DeepSeek's router aux-loss-free trick).
  4. Capacity factor. Tokens-per-expert cap: overflow tokens are dropped/bypassed — the memory-compute bargain's ugly edge.
  5. Why it works. Specialization emerges (code expert, math expert — though routing isn't cleanly semantic); sparse activation keeps inference cheap: inference-efficiency loves it, memory doesn't (all experts must be resident).
  6. Variants. Shared experts (one always-on), fine-grained experts (many small), expert-choice routing — the 2024+ design space.

Worked example

Mixtral 8×7B: $E = 8$ experts, top-$k = 2$ per token.

  • Total FFN params ≈ 8× dense; activated per token ≈ 2/8 = 25% — 47B total, ~13B activated: dense-13B speed, 47B capacity.
  • DeepSeek-V3 pushes further: 256 routed + 1 shared expert per layer, top-8 → 671B total, 37B activated — the recipe that made an open model trade punches with frontier closed ones.
