Accuracy vs thinking budget

A second axis of scale

Did you know?

Jason Wei's 2022 CoT paper showed "let's think step by step" unlocks latent ability — three years later RL-trained thinking is the product feature: every frontier model ships a slow-smart mode.

In the real world

o1/R1-class models spend thousands of hidden tokens per hard query — customers literally pay per thought. Reasoning tokens are now a pricing tier, not a trick.

Pretraining buys capability once; thinking spends it repeatedly per query. The panel's curve is the whole idea in one line: accuracy climbs as the model is allowed more tokens to think — same weights, more FLOPs at inference.

The chain is the scratchpad

Did you know?

A transformer can't store intermediate products between layers — but it can write them as tokens and read them back. CoT is working memory implemented through the output channel.

"17 × 24" fails in one forward pass but succeeds when decomposed to "17×20 = 340, 17×4 = 68, 340+68 = 408" — the model uses its own generated tokens as storage. RL then discovers which decompositions pay: backtracking, self-checks, casework.

Why math and code got there first

Did you know?

DeepSeek-R1's recipe — GRPO on math answers graded by exact match — produced spontaneous "aha" backtracking in the trace: the behaviour emerged because correct answers paid, not because anyone taught reflection.

In the real world

Extending this to unverifiable domains (writing, strategy) is the live frontier — rubric judges, process reward models and self-consistency are the candidates. See verifiable rewards.

A unit test can't be sweet-talked — that's the point. Dense, ground-truth reward lets RL shape long traces without reward-model blind spots.

What thinking costs, and what it hides

Did you know?

Turpin et al. (2023) showed models' stated reasoning can post-rationalize biased answers — the visible trace is not guaranteed to be the real computation. Reading the "mind" is interpretability's job, not the trace's.

In the real world

Every reasoning token is a served token — latency, KV cache, dollars. Inference efficiency decides how much thinking is affordable, and agents spend thinking to plan.

Test-time compute is a genuine axis — but it prices latency and cost, and the trace it buys is an engineered artifact, not a window. Both facts matter.
